# Findings

## Research
- The system needs to support multiple event types: Holidays, Stock Events, Macro Events, and Custom Events.
- Regions of interest: China A-shares, Hong Kong (HK), United States (US).

## Discoveries
- User wants a "visualized calendar view" generated by a local automated script.
- Key macro events explicitly mentioned: Fed rate cuts, US tax days, ADR custodial fee payment dates.

## Constraints
- Needs to be highly flexible to accommodate future event types.
